Xianghang (Scott) Mi

Founder & CEO

Dr. Xianghang (Scott) Mi is an Assistant Professor (Lecturer) in the Department of Software Systems and Cybersecurity at Monash University. He received his PhD in Computer Science from Indiana University. With over a decade of research in AI agent security, he has published over a dozen papers at top-tier security venues including IEEE S&P, USENIX Security, CCS, and NDSS, and received the NDSS 2019 Distinguished Paper Award. He was among the first to systematically uncover security vulnerabilities in voice assistant ecosystems (voice squatting, voice masquerading, command hijacking), and has achieved breakthrough results in efficient evasion attacks against In-Context Learning, federated learning, and vision models. Previously, he served as a Research Scientist at Facebook, a Professor at USTC, and an Assistant Professor at the University at Buffalo. TokenTorrent is founded to translate cutting-edge security research into deployable products and services.
